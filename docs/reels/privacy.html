Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Rimaz ("we") is a short-video app. This page explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, name and profile picture when you sign in with Google or email.
  • Content you post: videos, captions, channel details, follows and group memberships.

How we use it

To create your account, show your profile and channel, deliver content, and keep the service secure. We use only the basic Google profile scopes (email and profile). We do not sell your personal data.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored with our service provider Supabase. We share it only with providers needed to run the app, or when the law requires it.
