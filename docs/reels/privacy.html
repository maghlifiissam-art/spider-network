Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Rimaz ("we") is a short-video app. This page explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, name and profile picture when you sign in with Google or email.
  • Content you post: videos, captions, channel details, follows and group memberships.
  • Store accounts need a signed-in, email-verified account (Google or confirmed email). We do not collect ID documents.
  • Store data: your store name, handle, description, contact details and products, which are public. Likes, follows and ad view counts.
  • Payout details you choose to save in Settings (method, holder name, account such as an email, IBAN or phone, and country). They are private to your account, used only for future payouts, and you can delete them at any time. Payments are not active yet and no money moves.

How we use it

To create your account, show your profile and channel, deliver content, and keep the service secure. We use only the basic Google profile scopes (email and profile). We do not sell your personal data.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored with our service provider Supabase. We share it only with providers needed to run the app, or when the law requires it.
