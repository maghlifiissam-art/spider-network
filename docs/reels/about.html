A short video platform built for creators, viewers and beginners

RIMAZ is a new short video platform: watch reels, follow creators, open your own channel, publish series and stream live. RIMAZ is not a competing platform — the goal is more platforms and more income sources for everyone, alongside YouTube, TikTok and Facebook.

A creator-first revenue model

When the platform reaches 10 million views and 1,000 creators, RIMAZ begins distributing net advertising revenue: 70% to creators, proportional to views, paid monthly with a $50 minimum payout. Your content stays yours — original or properly licensed only, never any exclusivity.

Founding creators

The first creators to join receive an Early Creator badge, a personal invite link, their own channel, and AI-powered translation and subtitle tools so their videos travel across languages.
