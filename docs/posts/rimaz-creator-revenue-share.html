RIMAZ: the video platform that pays creators 70% of ad revenue

October 15, 2026 · Introducing the RIMAZ video platform

We launched RIMAZ, an international video platform built on a simple idea: the creator owns the value, so the creator takes 70% of the revenue their content earns. No paid membership to join, no complicated requirements — publish your videos and your earnings grow proportionally with your views.

How the revenue share works

1. A 70/30 split in your favor: from all net ad revenue the platform earns, 70% goes to creators and 30% keeps the platform running and improving.

2. Proportional to your views: your share of the creator pool is calculated from your views out of total views, transparently, with no hidden algorithms.

3. Monthly payouts from $50: once your balance reaches $50 it is paid in the monthly cycle; anything below rolls over automatically to the next month.

4. Original or licensed content only: to protect creators and the platform alike, we only publish content you own the rights to or hold a license for.

The full terms are public: rimaz.org/reels/terms.html.
