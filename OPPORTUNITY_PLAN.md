# Multi-track opportunity plan (big and small, all tracks)

Rules: free only, legal only, one real account per person per platform, real work only, no spend, nothing submitted without the owner's yes on the final entry.

| Track | Where | How we play it | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Big hackathons ($50k+) | Devpost, sponsor pages | One strong entry each, built on an idea we can finish solo with free tools; submit only if eligible from Morocco | check daily digest, enter 1-2 per month |
| Mid and small hackathons ($500-$50k) | Devpost, MLH, Dorahacks, Gitcoin | Highest volume. Reuse one core project across several; tailor the pitch per sponsor track | enter every fit, 3-5 per month |
| Grants and funds | fundsforNGOs, Grants.gov-type lists, startup/creator funds | Shortlist eligible, draft the application, owner reviews | weekly shortlist |
| Bug bounty (report-only) | HackerOne, Bugcrowd, YesWeHack, disclose.io programs | Only programs with a public safe-harbour policy and in-scope assets; passive review, no intrusion, no testing outside scope | account setup first, then weekly scope check |
| Beta testing | public beta programs, UserTesting-type | One real account, honest feedback | as listed |
| Design and content contests | public contests lists | Enter with our own original art | weekly |

Daily digest: opportunity_spider.py, tiers BIG/MID/SMALL, written to opportunities/. Owner is told only about items that pass an eligibility check.
Next steps: add more sources (MLH, Dorahacks, Gitcoin, design contests), eligibility check per item, then draft entries.
