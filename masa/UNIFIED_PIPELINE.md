# MASA unified pipeline (one line, three roles)

Goal: product choice is driven by data from all three roles, store content is built around the results.

1. SPY (competitors + ads): per shortlisted niche, record competitor SAR price, ad count/longevity (Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center), their angle and offer. Output: masa/data/spy-YYYY-MM-DD.md
2. HUNTER (opportunities + trends): trends and openings (seasonal Saudi events, trending sounds/products, gaps where few sellers). Output: masa/data/hunt-YYYY-MM-DD.md
3. BULLET (conversion): score each product = demand (spy) x gap (hunter) x margin at 120-280 SAR; pick top 1; fill the landing template, 3 short video scripts in Gulf Arabic, WhatsApp flow. Output: docs/masa/<product>/index.html
4. User approves the first product, then it goes live (free hosting, Tally form, WhatsApp +212663415542).
